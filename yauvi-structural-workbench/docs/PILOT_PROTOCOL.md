# Pilot and independent reproduction protocol

Prepared protocol, no participants enrolled and no outcomes recorded.

Recruit three computational researchers and three experimental biologists after
Yuvraj selects participants and approves contact. Obtain permission to retain
pseudonymous task notes; do not require confidential research inputs. An AI agent
is not an independent participant.

Assign one of the six workflows with a reviewed public or synthetic input bundle.
Ask each participant to install the exact wheel, create a case, assess readiness,
run, locate a measurement and its input mapping, explain missing evidence, export,
and reopen the report. Do not coach during the scored task. Record completion,
intervention, time, confusing language, outcome and the stated interpretation
limit. Separate usability failures from missing scientific prerequisites.

Targets: at least five of six complete their assigned workflow without developer
intervention; all six correctly identify its principal interpretation limit.
Any observed misunderstanding that turns geometry into biological proof blocks
pilot acceptance until corrected and retested. Targets are not measured results.

At least two external participants independently reproduce a reviewed bundle.
Record wheel and input hashes, protocol/reference identities, Python, OS, CPU,
external-tool versions, command, case-level outputs and justified numeric
tolerances. Preserve failures and discrepancies. Installation alone does not
establish scientific validity, and an external execution is not necessarily an
independent review of reference labels.

Use a local record with fields: participant_code, audience, date, artifact_sha256,
workflow, task_completed, interventions, interpretation_limit_response,
misunderstandings, environment, evidence_paths, consent_to_quote, follow_up.
No names, participation or consent should be invented.
